Generate 7 separate images, one at a time, each as its own standalone vertical 9:16 image (not a grid, not a collage, not combined into one picture). They are sequential keyframes of one fashion reel, so keep the same woman, garments, room, lighting and camera identical across all 7 images.

MODEL REFERENCE: use the attached model photo as the identity reference. Match her face, makeup, skin tone, hair color and hair length exactly as in the photo. Her hair is worn loose and down in every image and in both halves (no bun, no ponytail, no updo).

PHYSICS RULES (must be followed in every image):
- The skirt is one single piece of fabric with a fixed length that never changes. Worn at the natural waist, its hem just grazes the floor.
- When the waistband is lifted higher on the body, the hem rises by exactly the same distance. Lifting the waistband from waist to chest raises the hem from the floor to mid-calf: ankles and feet fully visible, no fabric touching or pooling on the floor.
- Fabric used for folding, gathering or draping over the shoulder is taken away from the length, so the hem gets shorter accordingly. No extra fabric appears from nowhere.
- Gravity applies: satin hangs straight down from where it is held or supported, folds settle naturally, nothing floats.

Every image uses this same setup:
- Layout: split screen divided down the middle by a thin seam. LEFT half = STYLING (the step listed below). RIGHT half = WEARING (unchanged in every image). Same woman in both halves.
- Garment: one bronze-brown liquid satin maxi skirt with high sheen and fluid folds.
- Setting: minimal bedroom, pale grey wall, low bed with white dotted bedding, dark wood plank floor, soft diffused daylight.
- Camera: static, front-facing, eye level, full body head to toe in each half.
- RIGHT half (WEARING), identical in all 7: black strapless tube top tucked into the bronze-brown satin mermaid maxi skirt worn at the natural waist, fitted at the hips and flaring so the hem grazes the floor, thin brown strappy heels, hair loose, standing straight, arms relaxed, neutral expression.
- Style: photorealistic, natural skin texture, realistic satin sheen. No text, no labels, no captions, no numbers, no watermark, no extra people.
